Try backlog per worker

A queue of 1,000 jobs means different pressure with five workers versus fifty. Each worker here processes one message at a time.

1,000 / 10 = 100 messages per worker. A 60-second wait budget / 2-second processing time gives a target of 30 messages per worker.

A steady-state sizing estimate, not an exact latency guarantee or deployable scaling policy. Startup time, in-flight work, bursts and per-worker concurrency affect real capacity. An ECS policy uses running task capacity; an EC2 ASG uses InService instances. Configure a scale-from-zero signal.
