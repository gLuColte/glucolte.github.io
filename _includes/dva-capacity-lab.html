Desired is a target; current is observed capacity

Min/max bound the target. A scaling decision changes desired capacity; the ASG then launches or terminates instances to converge. This model counts ready instances as current and shows pending capacity separately; it omits weights, cooldowns and replacement surges.

Lab range: 0–50 instances for readability; this is not an AWS quota. Dots = ready, dashed + = pending.

Current 3, desired 5: two more instances are needed. Editing desired by +3 gives 8, subject to max 10; current remains 3 until launches complete.

Important correction. “Current 3, desired 5, +3 → desired 8” is a desired-target edit, not a universal step-policy rule. A step scaling adjustment can be based on current ready capacity and account for warming instances: 3 + 3 gives target 6, with two already pending. Repeated alarms need not add three again. Scale-in is blocked while instances warm in this simplified policy mode.

Exam trigger: min 1 allows the fleet to shrink to a single instance; multiple configured AZs alone do not guarantee two healthy targets. ASG maintains capacity; ALB routes requests, with its own health checks.
