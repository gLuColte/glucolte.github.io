Legacy example. One eligible Linux instance × 24 h × 31 days = 744 instance-hours. Two under the same offer = 1,488 hours. Add usage across instances and Regions within that offer; the allowance is not per server or Region.

Free cost ≠ billing granularity. “0 seconds” is not EC2's general minimum billing duration. Eligible per-second billing has a 60-second minimum; an applicable allowance or credit can cover the charge. Check Billing → Free Tier / Credits for your actual eligibility, balance and expiry.
